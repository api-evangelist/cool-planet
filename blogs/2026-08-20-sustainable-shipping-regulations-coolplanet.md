Transformation in Shipping In recent years, the shipping industry has found itself amidst a transformative wave, steering towards a greener and more sustainable future. This transformation is largely driven by a series of stringent regulations introduced by the International Maritime Organisation (IMO), a specialist UN agency responsible for ensuring the safety and security of shipping and the prevention of marine pollution.
