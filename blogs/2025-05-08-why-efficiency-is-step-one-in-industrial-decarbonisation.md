When industrial manufacturers begin planning their decarbonisation roadmap, many jump straight to flashy technologies such as electrification of heat, hydrogen boilers, or battery energy storage . While these technologies are powerful tools, deploying them before addressing basic energy inefficiencies is one of the most expensive mistakes a company can make.
